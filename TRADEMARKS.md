# Project names and trademarks

This policy concerns project identity, not copyright or software licensing.
The repository makes no claim that “DeepPlant” is a registered trademark, and
does not use a registration symbol.

The AGPL licence permits copying, modifying, and distributing software under
its terms. It does not grant a right to represent an independent fork or
modified distribution as an official DeepPlant release. Please use a distinct
name and make the relationship clear.

Normal nominative references are welcome, including “compatible with
DeepPlant”, “fork of DeepPlant”, and “based on DeepPlant”, provided they are
accurate and do not imply endorsement or official status.
